Pro Tips
01Use the IP Calculator CIDR slider and spinner together — both stay perfectly in sync and update all results live.
02In the Subnet Splitter, enter a larger prefix than your current one — e.g. split a /24 into /26s to get four equal subnets.
03The Traceroute Lossy profile simulates degraded links with packet loss and high jitter — great for learning about real-world failures.
04VLSM always allocates largest subnets first. List your requirements in any order — the calculator sorts and aligns them automatically.
05In Route Summarization, coverage efficiency below 50% means the supernet is "loose" — consider splitting into two separate summary routes.
06The Bandwidth Calculator efficiency field defaults to 90%. Real TCP transfers over WAN often achieve 70–85% due to overhead and congestion.
07Every tool includes a Glossary tab with precise definitions — a complete on-demand networking reference built right in.
08All tools support PDF Export via the browser print dialog — choose "Save as PDF" to keep a record of any calculation.
